AgroPulse Intelligence is a precision-agriculture and environmental-analytics concept. We bring agricultural science, connected field data, and machine learning together to help growers and agricultural organizations make informed, resource-aware plans.

WHY WE EXIST

A connected view of every field.

AgroPulse Cloud Platform is the proposed platform behind the concept. It brings environmental, field-level, and spectral data into one view, then presents machine-learning insights in an accessible dashboard.

The aim is to help technical managers and field operators look beyond isolated measurements and consider the wider context behind crop, resource, and risk decisions.

OUR MISSION

Make useful agricultural intelligence more accessible.

Give growers and agricultural organizations accessible, data-driven tools that support crop planning, resource awareness, and the ecosystems agriculture depends on.

OUR VISION

Support a more climate-resilient way to farm.

Work toward precision-farming tools that are practical, scalable, and useful for farms of every scale — without losing sight of the people and ecosystems behind the data.

WHAT GUIDES THE WORK

Principles behind the predictions.

Technology is most useful when it serves people, supports careful decisions, and respects the systems agriculture depends on.

01 / STEWARDSHIP

Environmental stewardship

Support site-specific resource allocation with the aim of reducing unnecessary chemical runoff and conserving freshwater.

02 / ACCESS

Data democracy

Present complex machine-learning insights in an accessible interface for technical managers and field operators alike.

03 / RELIABILITY

Predictive reliability

Ground predictions in real or clearly labelled simulated data, and communicate what each model can—and cannot—support.

WHO THE PLATFORM IS DESIGNED FOR

Built around the people behind the food system.

Different roles need different views of the same field. The platform is shaped around the decisions each team is responsible for.

Farm enterprises

Coordinate field operations and resource planning across commercial acreage.

Grower cooperatives

Make data-informed tools approachable for smallholder and cooperative growers.

Agricultural insurers

Explore field signals and risk context for agricultural underwriting workflows.

Food distributors

Support supply-chain planning with a clearer view of crop and harvest outlooks.

WHAT THE MODELS DEMONSTRATE TODAY

Six models.
Three practical capabilities.

Held-out results on the project's own datasets — not field trials or independently verified performance. Demos trained on simulated data are labelled on their pages.

Yield estimation

Linear Regression estimates yield from full-season Sentinel-1 radar summaries plus crop and year context.

CYCleSS held-out · R² ≈ 0.66 · MAE 1.27 t/ha

Condition screening

Naive Bayes and SVM classify recorded crop conditions, and a CNN classifies tomato leaf photos.

PlantVillage subset · CNN ≈ 92% held-out accuracy

Resource guidance

A Decision Tree turns soil moisture, rain, and crop stage into irrigation advice. KNN compares new soil or plant samples with nearby known cases.

Decision Tree + KNN · interactive demos
